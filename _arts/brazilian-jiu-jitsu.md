## Historical Development

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu emerged from Japanese grappling instruction in Brazil, especially through figures connected to Judo and jujutsu. Over time, Brazilian practitioners emphasized ground control, leverage, and submission strategy.

## Technical Profile

BJJ organizes ground fighting through positions such as guard, side control, mount, and back control. The art rewards positional advancement and submission threats while allowing smaller practitioners to use leverage against larger opponents.

## Factbook Assessment

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is one of the most influential modern martial arts because it changed how many schools evaluate ground fighting. Its influence is especially visible in mixed martial arts, police defensive tactics, and recreational adult martial arts training.

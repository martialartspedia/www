## Historical Development

Karate emerged from Okinawan combat traditions often grouped under terms such as te and later karate. Its modern shape was influenced by public education, Japanese budo culture, and the need to teach large groups through standardized basics, kata, and rank structures.

## Technical Profile

Karate is usually identified with striking, but its historical kata also preserve close-range tactics, joint manipulation, throws, and body shifting. Modern training varies widely, from point sparring and kata competition to full-contact knockdown rules and self-defense applications.

## Factbook Assessment

Karate is best understood as a family of related systems rather than one uniform art. Its global identity is shaped by style, federation, ruleset, and whether a school emphasizes tradition, sport, self-defense, or physical education.

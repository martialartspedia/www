## Historical Development

Taekwondo developed in modern Korea through the consolidation and promotion of several kwan schools. Its international spread was shaped by state support, military instruction, and formal organizations.

## Technical Profile

Taekwondo is especially recognized for fast, high, and spinning kicks. Sport Taekwondo emphasizes distance, timing, and scoring, while traditional schools may also teach hand strikes, forms, self-defense, and breaking.

## Factbook Assessment

Taekwondo’s global reach is unusually broad because it functions simultaneously as a youth activity, Olympic sport, national cultural export, and martial discipline.

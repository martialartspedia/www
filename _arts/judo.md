## Historical Development

Judo was created during Japan’s modernization period, when older martial traditions were being reinterpreted for education, public health, and international exchange. Kano removed or controlled many dangerous techniques so students could train with resistance more safely.

## Technical Profile

Judo is built around throws, pins, chokes, and arm locks. Its technical engine is kuzushi, the disruption of balance before applying a throw. Modern sport rules influence tactics, especially gripping, posture, transition speed, and risk management.

## Factbook Assessment

Judo is a major bridge between traditional martial culture and modern international sport. Its methods influenced Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Sambo, mixed martial arts, police training, and many contemporary grappling programs.

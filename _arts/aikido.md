## Historical Development

Aikido emerged in 20th-century Japan from older jujutsu influences and Morihei Ueshiba’s broader philosophical development. Its public identity became strongly associated with harmony, control, and ethical restraint.

## Technical Profile

Aikido techniques often use entering, turning, joint control, and throwing mechanics. Training depends heavily on cooperative structure, safe falling, and body movement rather than competitive sparring.

## Factbook Assessment

Aikido occupies a distinct place in martial arts because its purpose is not primarily competitive success. Its value is usually framed through movement study, discipline, tradition, and controlled response to aggression.

## Historical Development

Although forms of fist fighting are ancient, modern boxing is strongly associated with British prizefighting, the Queensberry rules, weight classes, and later international amateur and professional organizations.

## Technical Profile

Boxing’s restricted toolset produces deep specialization. Footwork, rhythm, feints, guard positions, counters, and range control become highly refined because only punches are allowed.

## Factbook Assessment

Boxing is one of the most transferable striking systems for combat sports because it develops timing, distance, defensive awareness, and pressure-tested punching mechanics.

## Historical Development

Wrestling has no single origin point. It appears across ancient civilizations and continues through local folk styles, military training, school sport, Olympic competition, and professionalized entertainment forms.

## Technical Profile

Wrestling emphasizes control without necessarily relying on submissions. Depending on ruleset, success may come from takedowns, exposure, pins, escapes, reversals, or controlling an opponent’s body position.

## Factbook Assessment

Wrestling is a foundational grappling discipline because it teaches pressure, balance, takedown entries, defensive reactions, and physical resilience across many combat contexts.

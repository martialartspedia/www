## Training Role

MMA training requires integration. A boxing stance, wrestling shot, or BJJ guard may need modification because each range affects the others.

## Factbook Assessment

MMA has become a major testing ground for martial arts assumptions because it rewards techniques that survive under broad, resistant, multi-range pressure.

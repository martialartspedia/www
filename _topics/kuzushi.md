## Training Role

Students often learn kuzushi through throwing drills, grip exchanges, movement patterns, and live resistance. Without kuzushi, throws often become strength contests.

## Factbook Assessment

Kuzushi is one of the most important concepts for understanding why grappling arts work. It shifts attention from isolated techniques to timing, structure, and relationship between bodies.

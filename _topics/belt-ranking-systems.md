## Training Role

Ranks help schools divide curriculum into manageable stages. They can also create social expectations, teaching responsibilities, and organizational identity.

## Factbook Assessment

Belt rank is useful as an internal educational signal but unreliable as a universal comparison across styles, schools, or countries.

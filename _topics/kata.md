## Training Role

Kata can function as memory system, movement drill, teaching curriculum, and cultural preservation method. It is not always meant to resemble a literal fight from start to finish.

## Factbook Assessment

Kata is best understood as a transmission technology. Its value depends on whether a school connects solo movement to partner application, historical interpretation, and physical principles.

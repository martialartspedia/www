## Training Role

Clinch practice builds sensitivity to pressure, posture, and small positional advantages. It bridges striking and grappling ranges.

## Factbook Assessment

Clinch fighting is often underappreciated by beginners because it looks static, but it is one of the most technically dense ranges in combat sports.

## Regional Context

Japan’s modern martial arts were shaped by the transition from feudal combat traditions to modern education, nationalism, sport, and international cultural exchange.

## Factbook Assessment

Japan’s impact is institutional as much as technical. The dojo, belt ranks, formal etiquette, and federation structures became templates for many martial arts worldwide.

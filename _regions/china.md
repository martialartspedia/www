## Regional Context

Chinese martial arts are too diverse to describe as a single system. They include regional styles, family lineages, military methods, theatrical traditions, internal practices, and modern competitive formats.

## Factbook Assessment

China’s martial arts significance is cultural, technical, and historical. The challenge for reference work is avoiding overgeneralization while still mapping broad patterns.

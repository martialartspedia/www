## Regional Context

Muay Thai exists as sport, cultural practice, profession, and national symbol. Its development cannot be separated from Thai stadium culture and fight camp systems.

## Factbook Assessment

Thailand’s martial impact is concentrated but profound. Muay Thai’s technical transferability has made it one of the world’s most influential striking arts.

## Regional Context

Okinawa’s martial culture reflects its position between local traditions, Chinese influence, and Japanese modernization. Karate’s later global growth often obscures its Okinawan roots.

## Factbook Assessment

Okinawa should be treated as a distinct martial region rather than merely a subsection of Japanese karate history.

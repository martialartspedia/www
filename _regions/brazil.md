## Regional Context

Brazil’s martial identity combines sport, performance, self-defense, cultural resistance, and hybrid competition. BJJ and Capoeira show very different paths to global recognition.

## Factbook Assessment

Brazil’s impact on modern martial arts is disproportionate because BJJ altered global assumptions about ground fighting and Capoeira preserved a unique martial-cultural form.

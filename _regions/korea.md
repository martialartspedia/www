## Regional Context

Modern Korean martial arts developed through complex interactions between indigenous traditions, Japanese occupation-era influences, military systems, and postwar national promotion.

## Factbook Assessment

Korea’s martial arts influence is especially visible in kicking systems, Olympic sport organization, and the global spread of dojang-based instruction.

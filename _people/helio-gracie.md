## Biographical Context

Helio Gracie’s role is tied to the Gracie family’s promotion of jiu-jitsu in Brazil and internationally. The family’s challenge-match narrative became central to BJJ’s identity.

## Influence

The Gracie family’s visibility in early mixed martial arts helped bring Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu into global martial arts awareness.

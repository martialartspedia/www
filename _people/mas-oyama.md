## Biographical Context

Oyama’s public image emphasized toughness, discipline, and full-contact testing. Kyokushin developed a strong international network of dojos and competitions.

## Influence

Kyokushin influenced knockdown karate, Japanese kickboxing, Dutch kickboxing, and many full-contact striking methods.

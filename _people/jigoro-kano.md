## Biographical Context

Kano was an educator as well as a martial arts reformer. His central achievement was not simply founding a fighting style, but creating a repeatable institution that could teach physical, ethical, and social development.

## Influence

Judo influenced Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Sambo, police training, school athletics, Olympic combat sport, and the broader use of belt ranks in martial arts.

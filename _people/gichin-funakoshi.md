## Biographical Context

Funakoshi was part of the generation that reframed karate for modern public instruction. His writing and teaching helped present karate as a discipline of character as well as combat.

## Influence

Shotokan became one of the most internationally recognizable karate traditions. Funakoshi’s emphasis on etiquette, kata, and moral development shaped karate’s global image.

## Biographical Context

Ueshiba trained in several martial traditions and was deeply shaped by spiritual and philosophical commitments. His technical system evolved across his life.

## Influence

Aikido spread globally after the Second World War through students, dojos, and international organizations. Its influence is especially strong in discussions of budo philosophy and non-competitive martial training.

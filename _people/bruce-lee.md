## Biographical Context

Lee’s importance is both technical and cultural. He influenced how martial arts were represented in global media and how practitioners thought about efficiency, adaptability, and training across styles.

## Influence

Lee’s ideas anticipated later cross-training trends in mixed martial arts, although Jeet Kune Do itself remains a distinct and debated martial philosophy.
